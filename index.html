Privacy & connectivity. Messages travel directly between devices over an encrypted WebRTC data channel. QR/copy-paste is the signaling method; this page does not upload your offer, answer, or messages. A public STUN service is used to help discover network routes. Some restrictive networks require a TURN relay, which this server-free demo does not provide.
